PUB-04 Application Flow (Operational, Not Conceptual)

Step 01
Facility publishes Job Opening with public route and allowed fields.
Step 02
Candidate discovers openings by role, radius, facility tier, and contract type.
Step 03
Candidate opens detail and submits application via HRMS job form.
Step 04
Consent artifact captured with policy version and timestamp.
Step 05
System creates Job Applicant in private workflow queue.
Step 06
Recruiter receives candidate in New state for screening.

PUB-04 Privacy-By-Design Controls

  • PII minimization in public surface: no ID/license numbers before private workflow.
  • Consent scope displayed before submit with explicit acknowledgement.
  • Retention policy tag on each application for future anonymization.
  • No direct employee creation path from public workflow.

PUB-04 LinkedIn Publish Readiness

  • Posting fields map to HRMS Job Opening (title, location, employment type, compensation).
  • Partner-gated mode toggle controls external syndication.
  • Inbound applicants normalized to Job Applicant + consent capture.
  • Status sync delegated to integration worker (Phase B).
